--------------------------------------------------------------------------
DO NOT SUM CPUTimeRAW.  IT IS EXACTLY DOUBLE.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The accounting reports AllocCPUS = 2 for every task, although n13sc.slurm asks
for --ntasks=1 --cpus-per-task=1.  This cluster counts LOGICAL cpus, i.e. both
hyperthreads of the one physical core that --hint=nomultithread reserves.  Since
CPUTimeRAW = AllocCPUS x Elapsed, summing it gives 9,084,002 core-s = 2,523.3
core-h, exactly twice the truth.

Appendix A.4 defines core-hours as "the workers' running times summed", and
there is ONE single-threaded worker per task, so the figure is the sum of
Elapsed -- equivalently CPUTimeRAW / AllocCPUS.  Taking CPUTimeRAW at face value
would have overstated this row by 93%.

TotalCPU is 00:00:00 on every task: this cluster's accounting does not populate
that field for these jobs.  That is not evidence that no CPU was used, and it is
why Elapsed is the only field to trust here.  The same caution applies to the
15-vertex and order-12 campaigns if their costs are ever re-derived this way.
